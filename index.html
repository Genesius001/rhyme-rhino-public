Rhyme Rhino

Your Story — AI Vocals

Rhyme Rhino is an iPhone and iPad app that turns a voice note, a pet, a love story or a celebration into an original song with AI-written lyrics and AI vocals. Lyrics are free to create and edit; music is created with a subscription.
